## Interpretation and limits

**Verified from logs:** live plugin loading; position/stamina samples; sprint
observations and model exhaustion; pause/resume around the exercised states;
successful reload and continued sampling. The logs show resets across changes
in the recorded cell and sampling in the third cell after an interior transition.
Location names come from the user, not an independently resolved cell-name table.

**User-reported:** controls and gameplay felt unchanged; the checklist was
completed except mounting. We did not visually inspect the game.

**Not tested:** horse mounting, overworld travel outside Whiterun, death,
failed loads, new-game start, disabled observer mode, long sessions, other
runtime versions, and full frame-time overhead. Some unsupported-state pauses
can hide other simultaneous reasons; counts do not measure action frequency.

**Design limitation observed:** the model resets even when walking between
adjacent cells inside Whiterun. Twenty cell-change resets occurred. This follows
the conservative observer design, but repeated resets interrupt model depletion
and recovery. Before gameplay control, distinguish ordinary cell boundaries from
loading/teleports and test continuity explicitly. Do not remove lifecycle or
discontinuity protections blindly.

## Decision and next step

Accept the first observer milestone for this session's scope. No repeat of the
same checklist is required now. Keep the tested DLL/package unchanged while
planning the next step: continuous movement/stamina observation across ordinary
cell boundaries, then measured DDDA parameters and one opt-in control change.
